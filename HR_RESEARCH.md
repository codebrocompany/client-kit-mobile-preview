# HR letter research and review notes

These four documents are editable starting points, not legal advice or a
jurisdiction-independent compliance guarantee. They are intentionally neutral
and include India-relevant fields, but the employer must check the applicable
country, state, industry, employee classification, contract, standing orders,
company policy, and actual personnel/payroll record before issuing a letter.

## Document coverage

| Document | Included items | Required review before use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Employee Offer Letter | Employer and candidate identities, subject/reference, role, location, employment type, joining date, pay basis and frequency, benefits, hours, leave, probation, notice, conditions, acceptance and signatures. | Check whether a separate appointment letter or contract is required; verify compensation breakup, checks, work eligibility, policy links and exact offer conditions. |
| Relieving Letter | Verified joining and exit dates, resignation/acceptance references, last working day, effective release, handover, asset status, settlement status, authorized signature. | Match HR/payroll records; do not claim full-and-final settlement or completed asset return without confirmation. |
| Experience Letter | Verified service period, designations, team, location, role history, factual work summary, verification contact, signatory. | Remove unverified performance praise and confidential client/project information. |
| Termination Letter | Decision category and approved summary, process references, effective dates, notice/pay treatment, final-pay process, leave/benefits, property/access handover, contact and receipt record. | Do not issue from a template alone. Review reason, fair process, required notices/permissions, settlement, appeal route, privacy and delivery record for the actual case. |

## Primary sources consulted

- [India Ministry of Labour, Industrial Relations Code FAQ](https://www.labour.gov.in/static/uploads/2026/01/b9f5b3916c042a5597f9cb4f3cc9f0fd.pdf): explains that retrenchment notice, compensation and establishment-size permissions are context-dependent. The template therefore does not pre-fill one notice rule for every termination.
- [EPFO Joint Declaration SOP](https://www.epfindia.gov.in/site_docs/PDFs/Circulars/Y2023-2024/SOP_WSU_26032024.pdf): identifies appointment, resignation/termination, and experience/service documents as possible evidence for joining, leaving reason, and leaving date. This informed date/reference fields; it does not establish a universal letter format.
- [UK Government guidance on written employment particulars](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/employment-status-and-employment-rights/employment-status-and-rights-checklist-for-employers-and-other-engagers): confirms the importance of clearly recording core employment terms. UK requirements are **not** presented as Indian law here.
- [UK Government guidance on fair dismissals](https://www.gov.uk/dismiss-staff/fair-dismissals) and [dismissal process](https://www.gov.uk/dismiss-staff/how-to-dismiss-someone): used as cross-jurisdiction process checks for reason, investigation, communication, review and pay; the actual jurisdiction must be checked independently.

## Data and output limitations

Save Edits uses this browser's local storage, not an HR system. It is not
encrypted, shared across devices, access-controlled, or a personnel-record
retention solution. Avoid sensitive IDs, bank details, and allegations in a
shared-browser draft. Downloaded HTML, PDF, and PNG files must be stored and
shared securely. The page's note outside the document reminds users to verify
facts and get HR/legal review before issue. Printing hides editor controls and
uses white paper, but actual printer and mobile PDF output can vary.
