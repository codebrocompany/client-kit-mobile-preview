How to Use · Quick guide
Edit text
Tap any heading, label, value, or body text to edit it. Bracketed placeholders clear on focus.
Save your edits
Click Save Edits to keep your draft in this browser on this device. It does not sync to other devices. Use Download HTML for a portable backup.
Save as PDF
Click Save as PDF (or Cmd+P on Mac / Ctrl+P on Windows). Choose A4 or Letter, turn off browser headers and footers, then save or print. Documents use white paper even when your screen is dark.
Add your own content
Use + Section or + Field to add content. A red × removes its whole field pair, row, or section; Undo restores it. Upload a company logo with Add logo.
Switch theme
Use the Light mode button to switch the library and documents between dark and light. Your choice is remembered in this browser.
Save as PNG
Use Save as PNG for a single full-length image of the document. PNG and PDF use a light paper background for readability.
