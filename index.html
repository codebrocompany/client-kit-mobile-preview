How to Use · Quick guide
Edit text
Click any text directly on the page — it becomes editable instantly. Type to replace it with your own content.
Save your edits
Click Save Edits ↓ to download a filled HTML file with all your changes saved inside it.
Save as PDF
Click Save as PDF (or Cmd+P on Mac / Ctrl+P on Windows). Set paper to A4, margins to None, and enable Background graphics so the dark theme prints correctly.
Remove sections
Hover over any section to reveal the × delete button. Press Cmd+Z (Mac) or Ctrl+Z (Windows) to undo a deletion.
Switch theme
Use the ☀ Light button to toggle between dark and light mode. Light mode is useful for clients who prefer a clean white document.
Placeholders
All [BRACKET] text is a placeholder. Click to edit and replace with your business name, client name, or relevant details.
